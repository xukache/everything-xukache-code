# Fixed Article Assets

Use these bundled images as stable recurring article assets:

```text
assets/references/article-hero-watering.png
assets/references/article-footer-growth.png
```

## Purpose

- `article-hero-watering.png`: article opening image. 阿栩 waters a small seedling, representing patient AI learning.
- `article-footer-growth.png`: article closing image. 阿栩 stands beside the grown seedling, representing completed learning and accumulation.

## Rules

- Copy these files directly when the user wants the fixed top image or bottom image.
- Do not regenerate, crop, restyle, or add text unless the user explicitly asks.
- Save copied files under the article folder:

```text
<ARTICLE_DIR>/images/article-hero-watering.png
<ARTICLE_DIR>/images/article-footer-growth.png
```

- These are not shot-list candidates. They are fixed brand assets for article framing.
