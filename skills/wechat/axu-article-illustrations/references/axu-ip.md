# 阿栩 IP Rules

## Identity

- Name: 阿栩
- Brand context: Xukache 的 AI 圈
- Slogan: 不慌不忙，学点 AI
- Role: a professional, warm, restrained AI learning companion
- Personality: calm, patient, lightly friendly, reliable, curious, not authoritative or childish

## Visual DNA

阿栩 is a tangible simple character:

- warm-white rounded body
- soft charcoal outline and facial features
- small calm eyes
- slight understated smile
- balanced head-to-body proportion, not chibi
- small muted-sage leaf or top mark
- clean 2D hand-drawn flat style
- low-detail fill and minimal linework

Use these reference assets for identity calibration:

- `assets/references/core-character.png`
- `assets/references/expression-preview-128-v01.png`

## Color Rules

- Warm white body: dominant.
- Soft charcoal: outlines, eyes, facial expression, small labels if needed.
- Muted sage green: only the top leaf/top mark.
- Warm apricot: tiny accent only when needed; never blush or hearts.
- Mist gray: quiet shadows or separators.

Green must not appear on the body, limbs, clothes, scarf, chest mark, or props.

## Allowed Actions

阿栩 should act as the article's thinking companion:

- sorting scattered notes
- gently connecting two concepts
- watering or tending a small learning seedling when growth is the metaphor
- holding a light thread/path between steps
- placing simple blocks into order
- checking a small flow
- pointing attention through posture, not a pointer stick
- carrying one idea from input to output
- quietly observing a bottleneck
- sitting beside a structure when the structure is the focus

The action must be necessary. If removing 阿栩 does not change the meaning, redesign the image.

## Prohibitions

Do not render:

- text, logo, Chinese characters, or slogan on the standalone character body
- book, cup, computer, phone, tablet, pointer, chip, circuit, data stream, dashboard, code rain
- heart, blush, waving marks, sparkles, sticker-like emotion
- 3D, clay, plush, glossy material, realistic lighting
- cyberpunk, corporate blue tech style, Chinese traditional style
- huge eyes, oversized head, babyish limbs, exaggerated celebration
- green body, green clothing, green scarf, green limbs, green accessories other than the top leaf

Article illustrations may contain short Chinese labels in the environment, but never printed on 阿栩's body.
